Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

SkyPassage is built so that your data stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not run any server that receives your data.

Location

SkyPassage needs your precise location to find the aircraft near you and predict which ones will pass over you. The app sends your approximate position (latitude and longitude) to these public services, only to ask what is around you:

  • adsb.lol — live aircraft positions from the community ADS-B network.
  • aviationweather.gov (U.S. National Weather Service) — weather reports from the nearest airport.

These requests carry no name, account or device identifier. Those services receive your network address, as with any web request, and their own privacy policies apply. Location is used only while the app is open.

What stays on your device

  • Your logbook, recorded passes, alerts, saved spots and settings.
  • Sound Lab recordings, which are made only when you tap Record.
  • Airframe details the app learns from the live feed.

This data is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only to show the AR Spotter view; no photos or video are recorded or stored. The microphone is used only for Sound Lab recordings you start yourself.

Notifications, widget, Live Activity and Apple Watch

Alerts are local notifications created on your iPhone. The widget, Live Activity and Apple Watch app show the next pass that your iPhone already computed; that information is passed between your own devices only.

Children

SkyPassage does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
