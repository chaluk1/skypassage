No aircraft are shown

Live traffic comes from volunteer ADS-B receivers (adsb.lol). Some areas have little or no coverage. Near larger airports there is almost always traffic. Also check that Location is allowed and Precise Location is on in Settings → Privacy → Location Services → SkyPassage.

The look direction or AR labels are off

AR and heads-up views use the compass. Move away from metal and magnets, and calibrate by moving the iPhone in a figure of eight. Positions from the feed can be a few seconds old; SkyPassage extrapolates them along the aircraft's track.

Routes and runways

Routes come from published schedules for the flight number and are shown only when they match the aircraft's position and heading. Runways are inferred from the aircraft's track and shown with a confidence. Both are estimates, not official information.

Balcony and saved spots

In Balcony, tap Save this spot to set the part of the sky you can see from there. At a saved spot its view is used; everywhere else all of the sky above 10° counts as visible.
